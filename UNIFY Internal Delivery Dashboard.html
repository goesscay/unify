Go-live governance & delivery controls

1 · Baseline controlEvery project has an approved scope, schedule, budget, owner and change-control process.
2 · Stage gatesDiscovery, design, configuration, migration, integration, UAT and go-live require defined entry / exit criteria.
3 · Evidence-based sign-offConfiguration, migration reconciliation, test results, training, cutover and business approval are recorded.
4 · Escalation disciplineCritical risks have an owner, mitigation, due date, impact and escalation route.
5 · Resource governanceCapacity, allocation, utilisation and cross-project dependencies are reviewed regularly.
6 · Closure & handoverCustomer acceptance, support ownership, documentation and lessons learned are completed before closure.

Suggested go-live hard stops: material data reconciliation variance, unresolved critical defects, incomplete cutover / rollback plan, missing business approval or unassigned support ownership.
