# 12 — Go-to-Market Plan

## 1. Motion

**Product-owner-led, design-partner-first enterprise sales to ERP product companies.** Each customer is a strategic account: a product to connect, an implementation team to win over, and a CTO to satisfy.

| Stage | Months | Motion | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design partners | 0–7 | Product owners' networks; free adapter and pilot → paid | 1–2 product companies |
| Early adopters | 7–12 | Case-study-led outbound to regional/vertical ERP product companies | 3–5 paying |
| Scale ERP | 12–24 | Outbound + events + referrals; faster adapter onboarding | 15–25 paying |
| New categories | 18+ | WMS, logistics, CRM, manufacturing product companies | First 3–5 non-ERP |

## 2. Messaging by persona

| Persona | Message |
|---|---|
| CEO / founder | "Onboard 30–50% more customers with the same implementation team, and start billing weeks earlier." |
| VP Implementation | "Every implementation follows your best consultant's playbook: data migrated, setup verified and go-live evidenced, with no spreadsheet chaos." |
| CTO / product head | "Connect your product once through a governed, allow-listed adapter. No engineering backlog, full audit, sandbox-first." |
| Data migration lead | "Map in minutes, validate automatically, let customers fix their own data, and reconcile with full traceability." |
| The product company's customer | "One workspace to see what's done, what you need to do, and whether go-live is ready." |

## 3. Target list building

- Regional cloud ERP product companies in India (distribution, manufacturing, retail, pharma, construction, education verticals), GCC, SEA
- Vertical ERP/SaaS product companies with implementation teams
- Signals: job postings for "implementation consultant", "data migration specialist", "onboarding manager"; customer reviews mentioning long implementations; listings in software directories (G2, Capterra, SoftwareSuggest)

## 4. Channels

- **Product owners' networks**, LinkedIn outreach to CEOs and VPs of Implementation/Customer Success
- **Events**: SaaSBOOMi, NASSCOM product conclaves, regional ERP/industry trade shows; customer-success and professional-services communities
- **Content**: "The cost of a slow ERP implementation" benchmark report; implementation playbook templates; a free migration-readiness checker
- **Referrals**: investors and advisors in B2B SaaS; implementation leaders who move between companies

## 5. Sales process

1. Discovery call (implementation volume, duration, team size, pain) → 2. Demo on Odoo reference + their own anonymised customer file → 3. Technical session with the CTO (API readiness checklist, security pack) → 4. Pilot proposal (adapter + 2–3 customer implementations + baseline) → 5. LOI / pilot agreement → 6. Fortnightly pilot reviews → 7. Results review → 8. Annual contract.

Expected cycle: 2–4 months for mid-size product companies.

## 6. Customer success

- Joint implementation of their first playbook and knowledge pack (import their existing templates)
- Health score: implementations active, records migrated, customer-portal adoption, mapping acceptance rate, time-to-go-live trend
- Quarterly value review: hours saved, go-lives accelerated, revenue pulled forward
