# 12 — Go-to-Market Plan

## 1. Motion

**Founder-led, design-partner-first sales into ERP implementation partners**, then ecosystem-led growth (ERP marketplaces, vendor partner programmes), then a partner-of-partners motion via ERP vendors.

| Stage | Months | Motion | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design partners | 0–7 | Founder sells to their network; free pilot → paid | 2–3 partners |
| Early adopters | 7–12 | Case-study-led outbound + community | 10–15 partners |
| Ecosystem | 12–24 | AppSource / Odoo Apps / SuiteApp listings; vendor partner events; co-marketing | 50–80 partners |
| Vendor channel | 18+ | ERP vendors license UNIFY for their partner networks | 2–3 vendor deals |

## 2. Messaging by persona

| Persona | Message |
|---|---|
| Partner CEO / founder | "Deliver 30% more implementations with the same team, and protect fixed-price margins." |
| Head of Delivery | "Every project follows your best consultant's method: same playbook, same evidence, no surprises at go-live." |
| Data migration lead | "No more VLOOKUP marathons. Map in minutes, validate automatically, load and reconcile with full traceability." |
| End-customer sponsor | "See exactly what's done, what's yours to do and why go-live is (or isn't) ready." |

## 3. Channels

- **Founder network and LinkedIn**: ERP practice heads in India, the GCC and the UK
- **ERP communities**: Dynamics Communities (BCUG/DynamicsCon, Directions EMEA/ASIA), Odoo Experience, SAP B1 partner summits, NetSuite SuiteWorld
- **Content**: "ERP migration playbooks" (BC from Tally, BC from QuickBooks), templates, free mapping-checker tool as lead magnet
- **Marketplaces**: Microsoft AppSource (BC), Odoo Apps store, NetSuite SuiteApp marketplace
- **Partner referrals**: partner-to-partner, and vendor partner managers

## 4. Sales process (design-partner phase)

1. Discovery call (pain, project volume, current method) → 2. Workbench demo on *their* anonymised file → 3. Pilot proposal (90 days, 2–3 projects, baseline measured) → 4. LOI → 5. Weekly pilot check-ins → 6. Results review → 7. Annual contract at the design-partner price.

## 5. Customer success

- Onboarding: import their templates and checklists as UNIFY packs (white-glove at first, later self-serve)
- Health score: active projects, records migrated, portal adoption, mapping acceptance rate
- Quarterly value review: hours saved, projects delivered, issues avoided

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Website + waitlist + Trust page draft | Week 4 |
| First public case study | Month 8 |
| Marketplace listing (BC/Odoo) | Month 9–10 |
| Public launch (Product Hunt / ERP community events) | Month 9 |
